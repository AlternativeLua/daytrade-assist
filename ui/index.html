Trade on the demo account?

The app's signals will be placed as orders on your Pocket Option demo account, using the amount and multiplier set in the Paper account card.

Orders are only placed when your Pocket Option tab is logged in to the demo account, and only on pairs paying at least the minimum payout.

Trade with real money?

Every signal the app trades will be placed as a real order on your Pocket Option real-money account.

    Connect Pocket Option

    A small browser extension forwards the prices and payouts your logged-in Pocket Option tab already receives to this app. It never sees your password. It only places trades when you turn that on in the Paper account card, and only on the account you chose there.

    1. Open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode. If you loaded an older version, click its reload button instead and skip step 2.
    2. Click Load unpacked and choose this folder:
      —
    3. Open Pocket Option, pick an OTC pair and leave the tab open. Reload the tab if it was already open.
    4. The pair appears in the currency list here within a few seconds. Open more pairs in Pocket Option to add them.

    Keep it installed (Zen, LibreWolf, Floorp, Waterfox, Firefox Developer Edition or Nightly):

    1. Open about:config, search xpinstall.signatures.required and set it to false.
    2. Open about:addons, click the gear ⚙ → Install Add-on From File… and choose:
      —
    3. Allow the permissions it asks for: the Pocket Option sites, and this app on 127.0.0.1.

    Regular Firefox only keeps Mozilla-signed add-ons, so load it for this session instead. It is removed when Firefox restarts: open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox, click Load Temporary Add-on… and pick manifest.json in:

    —

    Then open Pocket Option and pick an OTC pair. If no prices arrive, open about:addons → Daytrade Assist bridge → Permissions and turn everything on.

    What the extension sees

    The extension's badge shows ON while it can reach the app. Keep this app running, since the extension sends to 127.0.0.1:8787. Pocket Option doesn't support third-party tools and may change its site at any time, which can stop the bridge working. This is for paper trading and research, not advice.
